CPU Scheduling Solver

A CPU Scheduling Solver is a tool or algorithm that determines the order in which processes are executed by the CPU. It applies scheduling algorithms such as FCFS, SJF, Priority, Round Robin, and SRTF to optimize CPU utilization, reduce waiting and turnaround time, and improve overall system performance. It helps visualize and compare the efficiency of different scheduling techniques.
